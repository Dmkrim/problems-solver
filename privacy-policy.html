Privacy Policy

App: Business Problems Solver (the "App"), by HiProgress. Effective date: November 5, 2026.

Summary

The App does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information you enter

Your problems, objectives, components, beliefs, solution ideas, ratings, goals, notes and checklist ticks are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. We cannot see them. If you use "Start over", delete a problem or use "Delete all my data", the data is deleted.

Pictures and documents you attach

You can take a picture or choose files. They are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. The App reads a picture or file only when you tap a button to add it.

Backup and save files

When you export a TXT file or a backup file, the App creates it on your phone. That file belongs to you. You decide where to keep it or whether to share it. We cannot see it.

Examples data bank

Once a day, when the phone is online, the App downloads a public file (data-bank.json) from the same website that hosts the App, to add new business examples. The request contains no personal data. Searches in the Adviser are made on your phone. Searches that found no example are listed only on your phone, and are shared only if you tap "Share my searches with no results".

Follow up links

The links you save stay on your phone. To show a small picture for each link, the App asks that website for its icon (for example apple-touch-icon.png or favicon.ico). This is a normal web request to the site you saved. Tapping Open opens the website in your browser; it has its own privacy policy.

Business tools, profile and sandbox

The business tools, your avatar, name, sandbox answers and settings are saved only on your device. PDFs and calendar files are created on your phone and are shared only when you choose to share them.

Sharing

The Share buttons send your PDF or a short summary only when you tap one. They open the app or website you choose (for example email, WhatsApp or Facebook), which has its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared automatically.

Permissions

The App does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone or messages. It uses the camera or your files only when you choose "Take a picture" or an upload button.

Third-party links

The HiProgress logo in the App opens our online shop (hiprogress.etsy.com) in your browser. That website is run by Etsy and has its own privacy policy.

Not professional advice

Solution ideas, scores and examples are for thinking and planning. They are not financial, legal or business advice. Example results are as described by their sources.

Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
